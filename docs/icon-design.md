# Application icon

Approved concept: flat GitHub Octocat holding a compact blue game controller,
with a white macOS tile, thin blue outline and orange controller buttons. Generated with
the built-in ImageGen tool. This fork is independent and is not endorsed by GitHub.

`Assets/app.png` supplies the sidebar and macOS packaging. `icon.ico` contains
the executable icon at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixels. The GitHub footer
link retains its original service icon.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: one preview concept for a macOS game launcher app icon.
Primary request: The recognizable GitHub Octocat mascot holding a video game controller with both front arms, as a playful clear symbol for a launcher of GitHub-hosted games. The Octocat identity must remain immediately recognizable: rounded cat head with two pointed ears, friendly simple face, small body and curled tentacle tail. The character is the main subject; controller is a small supporting object held in front of its body.
Style: clean, friendly, simple graphic icon with the restraint of a native Mac application icon. Bold silhouettes, smooth shapes, very subtle dimensional finish, little detail. Not an aggressive gaming mascot. Not photorealistic, not elaborate metallic armor.
Composition: centered complete Octocat holding controller, dark charcoal mascot with softly illuminated light face against a deep navy rounded-square app tile. Strong readable separation between mascot and background. Generous margin around the mascot, transparent exterior outside the tile. Tile face straight toward viewer.
Palette: near black and midnight navy, restrained electric blue rim accents, tiny orange buttons on the controller. Face light gray for readability. Controller simple and compact, blue D-pad and two orange buttons.
Constraints: single finished icon concept only, no text, no letters, no wordmark, no play triangle, no raven, no weapons, no glowing eyes, no presentation sheet, no extra objects, no Apple logo, no implication of official endorsement. Actual transparency outside rounded-square tile.

## Approved refinement

The final approved image uses a flat white background and a blue mascot outline
approximately one third as thick as the first flat concept. The black silhouette,
face, controller and orange buttons retain the same composition. Generated and
edited with the built-in ImageGen tool.

Final edit prompt: reduce the blue outline around the entire mascot, including
head, ears, tail, arms and tentacles, to approximately one third of its previous
thickness. Preserve the black silhouette dimensions, friendly face, oval eyes,
pose, blue controller, orange buttons, composition and scale. Keep the rounded
tile opaque white with a transparent exterior. Retain a flat two-dimensional
design without new shadows, glow, gradients, textures or objects.
